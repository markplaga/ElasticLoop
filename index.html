03 / THE LINE

How the loop works

A single closed strand gathers where the image is dark and spreads where it is light. Curves use differential growth: repulsion, tiny springs and smoothing let the line find its way. Squares grow, shrink and turn on a grid, always one cell at a time.

Still images grow, settle, then finish. Moving sources keep evolving. A spatial safety check protects the loop from crossing itself; separate subjects may stay connected by a thin bridge.
